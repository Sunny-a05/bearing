# Preferences

How you want AI agents in Bearing to behave when they're working with you. This is the config layer for your own workflow.

## Communication

- **Verbosity:** (terse, concise, detailed, narrative)
- **Ask me before:** (major changes, anything risky, nothing — just tell me what you did)
- **I prefer:** (bullet points, prose, tables, pseudocode, actual code, diagrams)

## Grilling (planning phase)

- **Question depth:** (surface-level gets the idea sharp, deep dives find hidden assumptions, mix both)
- **How many questions at a time:** (one at a time and wait, batch 3-5, just keep going)
- **What I want in the map:** (just decisions and unknowns, full reasoning, only open questions, everything)

## Research

- **Who do I care about talking to:** (users, competitors, domain experts, investors, all of the above)
- **Search breadth:** (focus on 3-5 sources, cast wide, depends on the question)
- **What "done" means:** (confidence > X%, found contradictions resolved, time's up, diminishing returns)

## Sources

- **Where I usually find things:** (web, academic, my own notes, conversations, books, Discord/communities)
- **What I trust:** (peer-reviewed, shipped code, firsthand experience, metrics/data, something else)
- **Red flags for sources:** (sensationalism, no accountability, vendor-written, age > X years, other)

## Dock & filing

- **When you find something, I want:** (the raw source, a summary + link, analysis of what it means, decision on whether to keep it)
- **Archive vs. wiki:** (err on the side of keeping everything, keep only what's immediately useful, I'll tell you)

## Escalation

- **I have Ollama installed:** (yes / no)
- **My preferred LLM when I need thinking:** (which model, or "whatever's fastest/cheapest/best")
- **Always ask me before:** (trying new tools, spending tokens on expensive models, making decisions)
